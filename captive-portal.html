4PHILLY
Know before you sign

Check any Philadelphia address before you rent it.

4PHILLY is a free, independent tool that pulls the City of Philadelphia's own public records for any address — so tenants can see the truth about a property in one place, in seconds.

What you'll see

  • L&I code violationsOpen and closed Licenses & Inspections cases — unsafe conditions, failed inspections, unresolved orders.
  • Rental license statusWhether the unit is legally licensed to rent, and whether that license is active, expired, or was never issued.
  • 311 complaintsWhat neighbors and past tenants actually reported — trash, heat, pests, illegal work — mapped to the address.
  • City Council districtWho represents the property and where to escalate if something's wrong after you move in.

How it works

  • Enter an addressAny Philadelphia street address — no account needed.
  • We query the City live4PHILLY reads directly from Philadelphia's open-data systems.
  • You get the full pictureViolations, licenses, and complaints in one readable summary.

Renters deserve to know what they're walking into. The records are already public — 4PHILLY just makes them easy to find and read.
