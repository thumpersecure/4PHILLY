# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 14, 2026

4philly.net ("the Site") is a civic transparency tool that aggregates and displays publicly available Philadelphia rental license and Licenses & Inspections (L&I) data. This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use the Site.

## 1. Data We Display

All property, license, and inspection data shown on this Site is sourced from public government records (e.g., the City of Philadelphia's open data portals and L&I databases). This data is public record and is presented here for civic transparency and research purposes. We do not independently verify the accuracy of third-party public records and are not responsible for errors in the source data. You can independently verify any record shown on this Site directly at the City of Philadelphia's official property lookup: [atlas.phila.gov](https://atlas.phila.gov/).

## 2. Data We Collect From Visitors

We aim to collect as little as possible.

- **No account creation.** The Site does not require sign-up, login, or personal profiles.
- **No sale of data.** We do not sell, rent, or trade visitor data to third parties.
- **Analytics.** We may use standard, privacy-respecting web analytics (e.g., aggregate page views, referrer, browser type, approximate location derived from IP) to understand usage and improve the Site. This data is aggregated and not used to identify individual visitors.
- **Server logs.** Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information (IP address, timestamp, requested page) for security and operational purposes. These logs are retained only as long as necessary for those purposes.
- **Cookies.** The Site does not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. Any cookies in use are strictly functional (e.g., remembering a display preference).

## 3. Third-Party Services

If the Site embeds or links to third-party services (e.g., mapping libraries, government data APIs), those services have their own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review independently. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

## 4. Contact Form / Correspondence (if applicable)

If the Site includes a way to contact us directly (email, form), any information you voluntarily provide is used only to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties beyond what's necessary to do so.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the Site and its infrastructure, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy or the Site's data practices can be directed to the site maintainer via the contact information listed on 4philly.net or the associated GitHub repository.
